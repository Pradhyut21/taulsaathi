# TaulSaathi: 10-minute buyer and seller feedback kit

**Status: prepared, not conducted. No participants, interviews, recordings or feedback results are claimed.** This kit does not contact anyone. Kannada text below is a draft for a native speaker to check before use. It does not establish native-language quality.

Test with a scrap buyer/dealer and a seller, together if practical. Record which role each person actually has; do not invent a second perspective if only one person participates. Use fictional transactions, their usual calculator/voice-note method and the local app. No money needs to move and no message needs to be sent.

## Before starting: one minute

Ask permission to run the exercise. Do not record audio or video by default. Ask separately if a recording is useful, and obtain separate permission before any public use. Names and phone numbers are unnecessary; anonymous IDs such as P01 are enough. Stop if the person wants to stop.

**English:** “We are testing a prototype that turns spoken weights and prices into a scrap receipt. Could you try it for ten minutes? This is a fictional transaction and no payment will happen. We want to learn where it is confusing. You may stop at any time.”

**Kannada draft:** “ಮಾತನಾಡಿದ ತೂಕ ಮತ್ತು ದರದಿಂದ ರಸೀದಿ ತಯಾರಿಸುವ ಮಾದರಿಯನ್ನು ಪರೀಕ್ಷಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇವೆ. ಹತ್ತು ನಿಮಿಷ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನಿಸಲು ಇಷ್ಟವಿದೆಯೇ? ಇದು ಉದಾಹರಣೆಯ ವ್ಯವಹಾರ; ಹಣ ಪಾವತಿ ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. ಯಾವುದು ಗೊಂದಲವಾಗುತ್ತದೆ ಎಂದು ತಿಳಿಯಲು ಬಯಸುತ್ತೇವೆ. ನೀವು ಯಾವಾಗ ಬೇಕಾದರೂ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಬಹುದು.”

Ask what they currently do when the bag has weight, a price changes, or the two people disagree. Write their actual answer in their words; do not suggest that the app is better.

## Two comparable tasks: five minutes total

Show only the inputs in the participant cards. Keep the answer key below out of view. Give one card to the usual method and the other to TaulSaathi. For the next pair, reverse the method order and card assignment. Record the order, since learning can affect time.

| Card | Material | Gross including bag | Empty bag/tare | Agreed rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | Paper / ಕಾಗದ | 27.5 kg | 1.5 kg | ₹18 per kg |
| B | Paper / ಕಾಗದ | 28.5 kg | 1.5 kg | ₹18 per kg |

**Task in English:** “Make a record both of you could check later. Agree on the material weight after removing the bag, the rate and the amount due. Use your normal method for this card.” For the second card, say: “Now try this app for the same kind of task.”

**Kannada draft:** “ನಂತರ ಇಬ್ಬರೂ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಬಹುದಾದ ದಾಖಲೆಯನ್ನು ಮಾಡಿ. ಚೀಲದ ತೂಕ ತೆಗೆದ ನಂತರದ ತೂಕ, ದರ ಮತ್ತು ಕೊಡಬೇಕಾದ ಮೊತ್ತವನ್ನು ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ. ಈ ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗೆ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ವಿಧಾನ ಬಳಸಿ. ಮುಂದಿನ ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗೆ ಈ ಆಪ್ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನಿಸಿ.”

Start timing when the card is shown. Stop when both people can identify the agreed net weight, rate and amount, and have a record they could refer to. For the usual method, allow a calculator, notebook or locally saved voice note, as they normally prefer. Do not force a method they never use.

For the app, let them speak naturally first. Count every explanation, retry and manual edit. If the bounded grammar needs coaching, show this example only after the first attempt and record “coaching needed”:

> Paper gross 27.5 kg tare 1.5 kg rate 18 rupees per kg.
>
> ಕಾಗದ ಒಟ್ಟು ತೂಕ 27.5 ಕೆಜಿ ಚೀಲದ ತೂಕ 1.5 ಕೆಜಿ ದರ 18 ರೂಪಾಯಿ ಪ್ರತಿ ಕೆಜಿ.

Do not claim natural speech support from a coached success. If live speech is unavailable, explicitly label the exercise “typed transcript interaction” and leave speech-quality observations unmeasured.

## Correction and trust task: two minutes

Give a new fictional card: paper, gross 12.5 kg, tare 0.5 kg. The buyer initially says ₹15 per kg, then corrects the final agreed rate to ₹50 per kg.

**English:** “The final agreed rate is fifty, after an earlier mistake of fifteen. Make the correction, check the revised receipt together, and save only when both agree. What tells you the old rate is gone?”

**Kannada draft:** “ಮೊದಲು ಹದಿನೈದು ಎಂದು ತಪ್ಪಾಗಿ ಹೇಳಲಾಗಿದೆ. ಕೊನೆಯದಾಗಿ ಒಪ್ಪಿದ ದರ ಐವತ್ತು ರೂಪಾಯಿ. ದರ ಸರಿಪಡಿಸಿ, ಹೊಸ ರಸೀದಿಯನ್ನು ಇಬ್ಬರೂ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ. ಇಬ್ಬರೂ ಒಪ್ಪಿದ ನಂತರ ಮಾತ್ರ ಉಳಿಸಿ. ಹಳೆಯ ದರ ಬದಲಾಗಿದೆ ಎಂದು ನಿಮಗೆ ಹೇಗೆ ತಿಳಿಯುತ್ತದೆ?”

Observe whether the correction is held for review, whether they find the edit, whether both people understand the readback and whether a new confirmation is required. A confirmation click alone is insufficient evidence that someone understood the values: ask each person to repeat the final net, rate and amount in their own words.

If time permits, say “the weights are in grams” or ask to cancel after a complete draft. Check whether they understand why review is needed. Do not run an actual disputed sale or ask them to accept a real amount.

## Neutral questions: two minutes

Ask these without suggesting the desired answer:

| English | Kannada draft |
|---|---|
| Which method would you choose for your next transaction, and why? | ಮುಂದಿನ ವ್ಯವಹಾರಕ್ಕೆ ಯಾವ ವಿಧಾನ ಆಯ್ಕೆ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀರಿ? ಏಕೆ? |
| Which weight or amount was hardest to check? | ಯಾವ ತೂಕ ಅಥವಾ ಮೊತ್ತ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಲು ಕಷ್ಟವಾಯಿತು? |
| What did the spoken number sound like to you? | ಹೇಳಿದ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ನಿಮಗೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಕೇಳಿಸಿತು? |
| Where did you need help or want to stop? | ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಸಹಾಯ ಬೇಕಾಯಿತು ಅಥವಾ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಬೇಕು ಅನಿಸಿತು? |
| What would make you distrust this receipt? | ಈ ರಸೀದಿಯನ್ನು ನಂಬದಿರಲು ಯಾವ ಕಾರಣ ಇರಬಹುದು? |
| What must change before you would use it? | ಬಳಸುವ ಮೊದಲು ಏನು ಬದಲಾಗಬೇಕು? |

For the **buyer**, additionally ask what record is needed when buying several materials and how price changes are agreed. For the **seller**, ask which values they need to see/hear to check payment and whether the record is understandable without the buyer explaining it. These are questions, not assumed pain points or findings.
